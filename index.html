Quick start

Use the WOC app to check in and book meals, keep allergy information updated, and view the tentative menu.

Tip (iPhone): Save the app to your Home Screen: tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

✅ Check-In & Book Meals

  1. Open the app with the APP LINK and enter your PIN.
  2. Tap Check-In & Book Meals.
  3. Select your dates and add people as needed.
  4. Choose the meals you want for each date (Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner).
  5. Tap Submit when finished.
Please note: If you are bringing guests, make sure their meals are included too.

⚠️ Member Profile – Allergies

Allergies are important for planning meals safely. Please select any allergies that apply to you or any guests you may have.

  1. Tap Member Profile.
  2. Find the Food Allergies section.
  3. Tap each allergy that applies (you’ll see them appear in the “Selected” line).
  4. Tap Save to store your selections.
Best practice: Keep allergies updated before your visit so the kitchen can plan ahead.

🍽️ Menu (Tentative)

The menu is provided as a tentative plan. Items may change.

  1. Tap the Menu tab.
  2. View upcoming dates to see what’s planned.
  3. If you don’t see anything yet, check again later.
Reminder: The menu is for viewing only — booking still happens in Check-In.
